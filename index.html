01 / about

About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Bahir Dar University in Ethiopia. Studying computer engineering has shown me how hardware, software and data come together, and it keeps pushing me to understand how things work under the surface, not only how to use them.

Programming is where that curiosity turns into something I can build. I enjoy web development and working with databases, and I like the satisfaction of taking an idea and turning it into a working project, even a small one.

Mathematics and logical problem-solving are a big part of how I think. They shape the way I approach data structures and algorithms, and they make debugging feel more like solving a puzzle than fixing a problem.

Outside of code, I'm a singer. Music is how I relax and stay creative, and it balances the logical side of engineering with something expressive.

I'm still early in my journey and I treat learning as a habit, not a phase. My goal is to keep growing my practical skills and to build digital solutions that are genuinely useful to the people who use them.
